# Research: Prayer Times and Athan

**Date**: 2026-11-02 | **Spec**: [spec.md](./spec.md) (rewritten 2026-11-02) | **Plan**: [plan.md](./plan.md)

This replaces the research for the first draft. Sources and measurements recorded then are reused
where still valid; the first draft's research is in git history.

## R1. Build on the existing code, reshape it to the new spec

**Decision**: Keep the working parts already on `008-web-app` and simplify the rest. Do not
restart.

| Keep (adapt) | Remove |
| --- | --- |
| `prayer_calculator.dart` with its polar-day and Asr corrections, Ramadan table, reference fixtures | `flutter_secure_storage`, `SecurePrayerSnapshotStore`, session-only mode |
| `prayer_location.dart` and the three Pages Functions (live on get.mthani.app) | Fallback acknowledgement (`acknowledgedFallbacks`, `system_notification: 1`) |
| `PrayerNotificationsPlugin.kt`, `PrayerAlarmReceiver.kt`, `PrayerAlarmStore.kt` (simplified) | Keystore AES-GCM encryption and claim tokens in `PrayerAlarmStore.kt` |
| iOS scheduling through `flutter_local_notifications` in `prayer_notifications_io.dart` | `PrayerMediaBridge.kt`, `PRAYER_ALARM_SPIKE`, `integration_test/prayer_alarm_spike_test.dart` |
| Web in-page alerts in `prayer_notifications_page.dart` | `ReminderState`, `ReminderClaimOwner`, requested-vs-effective mode split |
| `AthanPlayer`, `AthanDownloads`, notification-open host, Qiyam calculation | Four-value madhhab enum, `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` |

**Rationale**: Calculation, location services and native alerts have passing tests and live
endpoint checks. Most of the complexity to remove came from the first draft's exceptions (secure
storage, cold-start audio_service bridge, acknowledgements), which the new spec no longer needs.

**Alternatives considered**: Rewrite from scratch: throws away verified calculation and endpoint
work. Keep everything and add features: leaves code for requirements that no longer exist,
against Principle IV ("unused code MUST be removed").

## R2. Calculation methods, Asr, high latitude and adjustments

**Decision**: Keep `adhan_dart 2.0.1`. Its presets cover all 11 required methods:
`muslimWorldLeague`, `egyptian`, `ummAlQura`, `karachi`, `northAmerica`, `dubai`, `kuwait`,
`qatar`, `singapore`, `turkiye`, `moonsightingCommittee` (checked in the pinned package source,
`lib/src/CalculationMethod.dart`).

- **Asr**: two values, `standard` (library `Madhab.shafi`) and `hanafi`.
- **High latitude**: keep the two existing rules, middle of the night and one-seventh. The default
  follows the library's `HighLatitudeRule.recommended`: one-seventh above 48°, middle of the night
  otherwise.
- **Per-prayer adjustments**: applied by the app as whole minutes (−30..+30) after the existing
  polar and Asr corrections, then rounded to the minute. Not passed to the library's
  `adjustments`, because the calculator overwrites Fajr, Isha and Asr after the library returns.
- **Method adjustments**: Dubai, Turkey and Moonsighting Committee carry built-in minute offsets
  (`methodAdjustments`). The calculator's Asr, Fajr and Isha overwrites must re-apply them. The
  current code predates these methods and does not; fixtures for those three methods catch this.
- **Ramadan**: the existing versioned Umm al-Qura Ramadan table stays (Isha 90 → 120 minutes).

**Country defaults** (a suggestion, editable): EG → Egyptian; SA, YE → Umm al-Qura; AE → Dubai;
KW → Kuwait; QA → Qatar; TR → Turkey; SG, MY, BN, ID → Singapore; PK, BD, IN, AF → Karachi;
US, CA → ISNA; GB, IE → Moonsighting Committee; anything else → Muslim World League.

**Rationale**: No new dependency. Every method is a preset of the library already in use.

**Alternatives considered**: Exposing all 23 library presets: more choice than the spec asks for,
and each needs a fixture. Passing offsets into the library: lost by the calculator's overwrites.

## R3. Reference results for SC-002

**Decision**: Keep the existing PrayTimes-based fixtures (MWL, Egyptian, Umm al-Qura, Karachi,
ISNA) for the 10 cities. For the six added methods, take sample days from each authority's own
published timetable (Dubai IACAD, Kuwait Awqaf, Qatar Awqaf, MUIS Singapore, Diyanet Turkey,
moonsighting.com), two dates each including one daylight-saving or summer date. Record source URL,
date fetched and settings with each fixture in `tool/prayer/`.

**Rationale**: PrayTimes does not implement the six added methods. The authority's own table is
the most independent reference.

**Alternatives considered**: Fixtures generated by `adhan-js`: shares the algorithm family with
the library under test, so it is not independent.

## R4. Location: approximate only, and "My location"

**Decision**:

- **Android**: remove `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`; keep `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`. `geolocator` asks
  for `LocationAccuracy.low`.
- **iOS**: keep when-in-use only, add `NSLocationDefaultAccuracyReduced = true` so the system
  prompt offers approximate location by default.
- **Web**: `getCurrentPosition` with `enableHighAccuracy: false`.
- **Rounding**: every position is rounded to 2 decimal places (≈1.1 km) before it is saved or
  sent off the phone. One kilometre east or west moves times by about 4 seconds.
- **"My location" refresh**: after the first reader frame, when the app starts or resumes, and at
  most once per 30 minutes. It runs only if the current place is "My location" and location
  permission is already granted (checked without prompting). If the rounded position moved more
  than 10 km from the stored one, call `/api/prayer-resolve`. If offline or on failure, keep the
  previous place and retry on the next resume. A changed city sets a one-time "moved to {city}"
  message shown on Prayer Times.
- **Method when travelling**: "My location" keeps a method the reader chose. If the method is
  still the automatic suggestion, it is updated to the new country's suggestion.

**Rationale**: Prayer times need no better than city-level accuracy. Dropping precise location
removes the reason for secure storage (R6) and simplifies store privacy answers. Refreshing on
resume gives travellers correct alerts without background location.

**Alternatives considered**: Background or significant-change location: needs "always" permission
and battery for no gain over refresh on open. Precise location rounded afterwards: still prompts
for precise access, which the spec forbids.

## R5. City search and the internet-based estimate

**Decision**: Keep the three Pages Functions unchanged (GeoNames `searchJSON`, `findNearbyPlaceNameJSON`
+ `timezoneJSON`, and Cloudflare `request.cf`). Change only the client:

- The estimate (`GET /api/prayer-location`) is requested when city search opens, online, and
  "Use my location" is unavailable or refused. It appears as the first row: "Suggested: Cairo,
  Egypt — estimated from your internet connection". Tapping it selects it.
- `POST /api/prayer-resolve` receives already-rounded coordinates.

**Rationale**: The endpoints passed live checks for the ten reference cities on 2026-10-29. A
suggestion row replaces the first draft's separate disclosure-and-confirm flow, as the spec now
asks.

**Quota**: GeoNames free capacity is 10,000 credits a day and 1,000 an hour. Search costs 1,
resolve costs 4. Resolving only on a move over 10 km keeps "My location" cheap. Watch usage after
release. Paid capacity needs only a configuration change.

**Alternatives considered**: Bundling a city and time-zone database: several MB, against
Principle IV. A third-party IP provider: unnecessary with `request.cf`.

## R6. Storage

**Decision**: Store prayer state as one JSON value under the key `prayer.state` in the existing
`preferences` table of `user.db` (`lib/data/user_repository.dart`), with schema version 2. Remove
`flutter_secure_storage`. Parse the value lazily, only when Prayer Times or the alert refill
needs it.

No migration from the version 1 encrypted snapshot. No store release contains prayer code (1.5.0
predates it). Web users since 2026-10-29 set their place again. On web, delete the leftover
`flutter_secure_storage` localStorage entries.

**Rationale**: The spec's assumption: city-level positions are not sensitive data under the
constitution's secure-storage rule. `user.db` already works on iOS, Android and web.

**Alternatives considered**: Keep secure storage: needs a web exception and more failure states
for no gain at city level. `shared_preferences`: a new dependency when `user.db` already exists.

## R7. Alerts on each platform

**Decision**: Dart computes the next 60 alerts for the current place (≈10 days at 6 a day,
meeting the 7-day requirement) and hands them to the platform. Platforms receive a full
replacement list each time. Refills happen on every change and on app start or resume after the
first frame.

- **Android**: keep the existing plugin, receiver and alarm store. One
  `setExactAndAllowWhileIdle` alarm per alert, restored on boot, time change and exact-alarm
  permission change. The store becomes plain `SharedPreferences` JSON. Each alert carries a
  generation number, and the receiver ignores alerts from an older generation.
  - If exact alarms are not allowed: use `setAndAllowWhileIdle` and show "alerts may be a few
    minutes late" with a button to allow them.
  - Alerts more than 15 minutes late are dropped as missed. The first draft dropped anything
    over 60 seconds late, which silently lost Doze-delayed alerts.
- **iOS**: `zonedSchedule` with absolute UTC times, a maximum of 60 pending of the 64 allowed,
  and only app-owned requests cancelled.
- **Web**: page timers while open (existing adapter). No push.

**Rationale**: Same data model on all platforms. All of these parts exist and have tests.

**Alternatives considered**: A chained single Android alarm: fewer alarms but a broken chain
loses all later alerts. Web Push: needs a server and still cannot play an athan.

## R8. Sounds

**Decision**: Sound choices are the recitations in the catalogue, `tone` and `silent`.

- **Opening clips**: bundled natively per platform so each store package carries one format.
  Android: `android/app/src/main/res/raw/athan_<id>_opening.ogg`. iOS: `ios/Runner/athan_<id>_opening.caf`
  as a bundle resource, under 30 s. Not Flutter assets, which would ship both formats on both
  platforms.
- **Tone**: the existing generated `qiyam_tone` serves both prayer "simple tone" and Qiyam.
- **Full recordings**: downloaded on demand from get.mthani.app by the existing `AthanDownloads`
  (size and SHA-256 check). Until downloaded, full mode behaves as opening mode and says so
  (FR-023).
- **Android channels**: channel sound is fixed once created, so there is one channel per sound:
  `prayer_<soundId>_v1`, plus `prayer_silent_v1` and the existing Qiyam channel. The user's
  channel settings are never overridden.

**Rationale**: Bundled opening clips are available offline from install, so FR-023's fallback is
needed only for full recordings. Size is about 150–250 KB per clip per platform.

**Alternatives considered**: Downloaded opening clips: Android channels cannot reliably use
app-private files, and iOS needs copying to Library/Sounds.

## R9. Full athan

**Decision**:

- **Android**: a small native foreground service, `AthanPlaybackService.kt`, of type
  `mediaPlayback`.
  - **Start**: the exact-alarm receiver starts it when the mode is full and the recording is
    downloaded. Starting a foreground service from an exact alarm is an allowed background-start
    exemption.
  - **Playback**: `MediaPlayer` plays the file, holding permanent audio focus
    (`AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN`). The service shows a notification with Stop and stops itself at the end.
  - **Quran**: recitation (`just_audio`, `handleInterruptions: true`) sees a permanent focus loss.
    It pauses and does not resume, as FR-026 requires.
  - **Memorization**: if the app process is alive, the plugin sends `athanStarted` to Dart, which
    pauses memorization listening.
  - **Fallback**: without exact-alarm access, the service is not started and the opening-clip
    alert is posted instead.
- **iOS**: in-app playback through the existing `AthanPlayer` (`just_audio`).
  - When an alert arrives with the app open and full mode on, the foreground handler hides its
    sound and the app plays the full recording.
  - When the reader taps an alert, Prayer Times opens and plays the full recording.
- **Web**: in-page `AthanPlayer` while the page is open.

**Rationale**: The first draft's route, exact alarm → cold Flutter engine → `audio_service`
custom action (G01), was never proven on a device and was the riskiest part of the design. A
native service does not need a Flutter engine at alarm time.

**Alternatives considered**: Routing through `audio_service` (G01): complex and unproven, kept
out. Chained notifications or silent audio on iOS: against platform rules.

## R10. Athan recordings

**Decision**: The catalogue ships empty until at least two recordings have written
redistribution permission. Each entry needs the full file, an opening cut after the fourth
"Allahu akbar", and a recorded source, licence and reciter. Status on 2026-11-02:
- **Al-Husary** (Assabile): reviewed by the user; opening cut 0:00–0:12; rights unconfirmed.
- **Second recording**: none. The El-Minshawi candidate was rejected as misattributed.

Ways to clear rights: written permission from the rights holder, recordings under an explicit
open licence (CC0 / CC BY), or commissioning a muezzin. The asset pipeline
(`tool/prayer/prepare_athan.py`) is reused.

**Rationale**: Stories 1–3 and 6, and Story 4 with the tone, do not depend on recordings. FR-019
and Story 5 do.

## R11. Startup and performance

**Decision**: Nothing prayer-related runs before the first reader frame. After the first frame,
if prayer state exists, one background task:
1. Parses the stored JSON.
2. Loads the time-zone database (`latest_all.dart`). *Checked during implementation
   (2026-11-02):* `latest_10y.dart` lacks 253 of the 598 zone names, all of them link zones
   such as `Europe/Oslo` and `Africa/Accra`. GeoNames returns these names, so the smaller
   database was rejected.
3. Refills alerts.
4. Refreshes "My location" if applicable.

**Rationale**: Principle III. Alerts must be refilled even when the reader never opens Prayer
Times. Measure the task on the Galaxy A15 to confirm it does not affect scrolling.

**Alternatives considered**: Refill only when Prayer Times opens: alerts would stop after 10 days
for readers who use only the Mushaf.

## R12. Store privacy

**Decision**: Update the App Privacy and Data safety answers before release:
- **Approximate location**: collected for app functionality, not linked to identity, not used
  for tracking, processed ephemerally by Mathani's server and GeoNames.
- **Precise location**: no longer requested.
- **Docs**: update `docs/store/privacy.md` and the published privacy policy.
